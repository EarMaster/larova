## The short version

**The app collects nothing.** Larova has no account system, no analytics, no crash reporting and
**no internet permission at all** — not a setting you can turn off, but a permission the app does
not declare, which Android enforces. Everything you write stays on the device it was written on.
Nothing you put into Larova is ever sent to the developer, because there is no server to send it
to.

**This website is separate and is described below.** It exists mainly because Google Play requires
a reachable privacy policy URL. It sets no cookies, loads nothing from third-party services, and
has no analytics.

## Who is responsible

Nico Wiedemann, the developer of Larova, is the controller for this website under the GDPR.
Contact: [privacy@larova.app](mailto:privacy@larova.app).

For the content you create inside the app, **you** are the one who decides what happens to it. The
developer never receives it and cannot access it, so there is no processing to be responsible for.

## The app

### What Larova stores, and where

Everything you create — tiles, guides, notes, checklists, tables, contacts, photos, videos, voice
recordings — is stored in the app's private storage on the device. Photos and recordings are held
as files in that private area; everything else in a local database. Other apps cannot read it, and
it is deleted when Larova is uninstalled.

### What Larova sends

Nothing. The app declares no internet permission, so it cannot make a network request even if a
future bug tried to. There is no telemetry, no usage statistics, no crash reports, no advertising
identifiers and no third-party SDKs.

### Permissions the app asks for

- **Microphone** — only when you record a voice note, and only for as long as you are recording.
- **Camera** — only when you take a photo or video from within the app.
- **Notifications** — only if reminders are enabled.

All three are used on the device for the feature you asked for. Nothing captured through them
leaves the device. Larova asks for no location permission, no contacts permission, and no storage
permission — pictures and videos are chosen through the Android photo picker, which hands the app
one file at a time rather than access to your gallery.

### Phone calls

Larova never dials. Tapping a contact opens Android's own phone app with the number filled in, and
the person holding the phone decides whether to place the call. The app has no permission to dial
on its own, and it never transmits a location.

### The activity log

Larova notes locally when a tile was opened and when a checklist item was ticked, so a parent can
look back in the evening. Caregivers can add their own entries. This log never leaves the device,
is kept for 30 days by default, can be shortened or cleared at any time in settings, and is a plain
list of events — the app does not score, rate, interpret or draw conclusions from it.

### Backup and handover files

Exporting produces a single file containing your tiles and media. **You** choose where it goes,
through Android's own file dialog — the device itself, a USB stick, or a cloud provider you already
have installed.

If you choose a cloud destination, that provider's own privacy policy governs what happens to the
file from then on. Larova has no integration with any cloud service and no knowledge of where the
file went. You can protect the file with a password, which encrypts its contents; we recommend
doing so whenever the file leaves your hands. **A password you lose cannot be recovered** — there
is no key escrow and no reset, because that would require a server.

### Health information and other sensitive notes

Anything you write about a child's health, needs or behaviour may count as a special category of
personal data under Article 9 GDPR. The local-only design is the answer to that: it is not
transmitted, not processed by any operator, and no data processing agreement is needed, because
nobody but you and the people you hand the phone to ever sees it.

Larova is a notebook. It does not diagnose, does not calculate doses, does not interpret what you
write and does not give advice. It is not a medical device and not an emergency service.

### Children

Larova is set up and read by adults, even though it usually lives on a child's device. It is not
directed at children, carries no advertising, and has no in-app purchases or social features.

## This website

### Hosting and server logs

The site is served by **GitHub Pages** (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web server, GitHub's
infrastructure processes technical data needed to deliver the page — including your IP address,
the time of the request, the page requested and your browser's user agent. This happens in
GitHub's systems, not ours; we have no access to those logs and cannot connect them to a person.
The legal basis is our legitimate interest in serving a working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See
the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

### No cookies, no analytics, no third parties

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, tag managers, tracking pixels or embedded social
media. Every asset — stylesheet, icon, image — is served from this domain. In particular **no web
fonts are loaded from an external service**: the page uses the fonts already on your device.

### Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection
regarding personal data we hold, and the right to complain to a supervisory authority. In practice
we hold nothing about you: the app sends us nothing, and the website's server logs sit with GitHub
rather than with us. If you write to the address above, we will hold your message for as long as
needed to answer it.

## The Play Store

Downloading Larova from Google Play is a transaction between you and Google, governed by the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Google may tell us aggregate,
anonymised install and crash statistics through the Play Console. We do not receive personal data
from Google, and Larova itself reports nothing to it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version appears on this page with a new date. The app does not
check for changes, and cannot, so material changes will also be noted in the release notes.
